12:15 to 12:45 · PROTECTED TEAM WORK

Thirty minutes on your board

Use your existing shared board. These phases can help you get started; move between them at your team’s pace.

30:00

Optional silent timer · press Start when team work begins.

Make a visual decision together

01 / AGREE

Name the experience

State how people need each other, then the feeling or world you want a player to recognize. Choose one visual decision to work on.

02 / CURATE

Make references earn their place

Choose, compare, arrange, and revise images. What does each reference help a teammate make? Keep useful source links and labels with the board.

03 / INSPECT

Choose what to test next

Look at the board together. Replace an outlier, clarify a palette relationship, or name an unresolved choice you want feedback on.

A coherent section and an explained choice are useful progress today. There is no new image quota or submission for this work block.

Optional feedback during your work

When useful, ask a teammate or your instructor what feeling or world the board communicates before explaining it. Keep working within your team; there is no whole-room share-back during this block.

I Like

A specific strength, with a reference you can point to.

I Wish

A named gap, mismatch, or point of confusion.

What If

A possibility the makers could test.

Before the block ends: save your work

Act on one useful piece of feedback. Record the reason for the change, who does what next, and when you will compare the work together. Confirm everyone can access the board.
