For the room · a two-minute demonstration
  1. Use Test sound and adjust the room volume. Play On time; ask the room to name the visible contact.
  2. Play Sound early, then Sound late. The action, six repetitions and sound are identical. Only sound timing changes.
  3. Ask for an observable difference first, then an interpretation. Different emotional readings can share the same evidence.
  4. Use silent inspection to stop exactly at contact and compare the two event markers.
