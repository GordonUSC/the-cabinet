What your frame gives us

The relationship in space and both gestures are available together.

What it withholds

Faces occupy less of the image. Small facial details may be harder to read.

Try a moment and a framing. You can keep up to three choices to compare.

The difference we can check

Framing changes how much of the performance is visible. The performance itself does not change when you switch cameras. The named moments do change the pose, so compare frames within one moment first.

Let the evidence lead.

Three tools for the next conversation.

Blocking

The arrangement and movement of performers within a space. Ask who can see, approach or avoid whom.

Gesturality

How bodily gestures and movement create expressive meaning. A repeated gesture may become part of a character’s recognizable behavior.

Reaction shot

A shot showing a response to another action or line, often redirecting attention to someone who is not speaking.

Bring one observation, not a verdict.

“I noticed ___ more than once. It might suggest ___. I would need ___ before being sure.”
