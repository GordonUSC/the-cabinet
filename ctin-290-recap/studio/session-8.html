Words that earn their place.

Tone

The emotional or stylistic character of a work, shaped by image, performance, sound and pacing. Name choices that support your reading rather than treating a feeling as a universal fact.

Exposition

Information that helps us understand a story’s world, characters or situation. It may arrive through dialogue, images, action or environmental detail.

Sync point · practice helper

A moment when a visual event and an audible event align. The marked beat and landing let you inspect one.

Thesis · practice helper

A specific claim that your examples can support, question or refine. “It looks good” needs a more precise claim.
