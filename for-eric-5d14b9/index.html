A roadmap for Eric Francksen, producer of rooms where people belong

A needle drop, then the band. Nothing plays until you press.

Eric, you already do the thing most people only post about: you make room. You welcome strangers by name, you celebrate the participants first, and you say "First, breathe" when someone needs it.

This roadmap helps the right people find that, on purpose, in public, a few minutes a week.

Who you're calling in

The Invitation

Five traits, written as the welcome you would say at the door.

  • CourageIf you try new things in front of people, you belong here.
  • InclusivityIf you save a seat for the person who just walked in, pull up a chair.
  • Open-mindednessIf you would rather ask than assume, there is a game here you have never played.
  • VulnerabilityIf you can name the hard part out loud, you are among friends.
  • Lifting others upIf you light up when someone else wins, this table is yours.

The Roadmap

Four movements, one per step of your retro. The line below is drawn to scale: one year, every milestone a diamond. Tick them off as you go.

I
Movement I

First, Breathe

October 26 to November 8, 2026 · set the stage
  • Identity kit. A one-line bio, one handle everywhere, a photo you choose, and your emblem credited to you.
  • Two home channels. LinkedIn for the professional room, Instagram for the warm and visual one. Discord stays the community room.
  • Your why, in three sentences.
II
Movement II

Celebrate the Participants

November 9 to December 20, 2026 · the weekly habit
  • Every Monday: a "Celebrate the participants" post naming and thanking the real people behind one thing (GGP Connect, a game night, a collaborator). Your retro format.
  • Every Wednesday: lift three people. Comment, share, or introduce two people who should meet.
  • Every Friday: a First. Something you tried, what was hard, what you learned.
III
Movement III

Gather Data, Explore Learnings

December 21, 2026 to February 14, 2027 · find your people
  • Monthly Open Table, second Thursday (January 14, February 11). Invite three new people by name each time.
  • Producer's Notes, a mini series: one practical tip a week on making room. Retros, onboarding, a welcome script.
  • 90-day retro on January 24, in your four-step format, shared publicly: what worked, who showed up.
IV
Movement IV

Dream of What's Next

February 15, 2027 onward · take the table on the road
  • GDC week, March 1 to 5: host an in-person Open Table, be visible at the Directory launch and Zero Proof Game Night, and meet 10 people from the community in person.
  • June, Pride: a Pride Open Table. Share one first of your own, only what you choose.
  • August: your dream, streamed to lift GGP Scholars. In your words: "a remote top tier RPG one off with leaders in the industry" (August 8, 2025).
  • October 26, 2027: the one-year retro. Celebrate the participants.
