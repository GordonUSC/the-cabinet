What this suggests

Collaboration is where the people are

The same group drew 14 times more sign-ups when it co-hosted than on its own. Sign-ups are not attendance, but the gap is large.

Ginsburg Hall is the shared room

Four different hosts use it this month. They share a building, and nothing public shows them sharing a program.

AI for Media and Storytelling, the Cinematic Arts and Annenberg initiative, co-presented the Flux Festival in April 2026 and runs six faculty working groups, none of them about games.
