Applicant review rubricv2tap to open

Five criteria, 1 to 4 each. Score = weight x level / 4, top is 100. The weights are a starting point; change them if they feel wrong.

Makes things
25

any medium: game, mod, level, art, code, writing, jam entry

  1. 1Wants to make things; nothing shown
  2. 2Names one thing made; no link or detail
  3. 3Shows one finished thing and says what they did
  4. 4Several finished things, or one shipped with others, with their part named
Lifts others
25

GGP values in action: belonging, serving underestimated communities, collaboration

  1. 1No example of helping anyone
  2. 2A one-off example
  3. 3A repeated role: runs a club night, tutors, moderates
  4. 4Started or grew something others rely on, and says who it helped
Growth story
20

where they are going and what this year unlocks

  1. 1Generic: “I love games”
  2. 2A direction, no next step
  3. 3A direction plus one concrete next step
  4. 4Direction, next step, and how tours, reviews, GDC and peers get them there
Shows up
15

follow-through

  1. 1Missing required parts
  2. 2Complete, thin answers
  3. 3Complete, every prompt answered with specifics
  4. 4Specific, plus proof of finishing things elsewhere
Will give back
15

mentor, host, help the next class

  1. 1Not mentioned
  2. 2Says they would like to
  3. 3Names how they would help
  4. 4Already helps newer people and says so
Try it: score a fictional applicant

Total 0 / 100

Bias checks
  • Video equals essay. Scored on the same anchors. Transcribe so it reads like text.
  • No AI detector decides. Detectors flag plain and non-native writing far more often. If something reads off, have a 15 minute conversation.
  • No points for polish. No credit for school name, vocabulary or fancy formatting. Score what they did.
  • Names hidden first read. Where Kaleidoscope allows. Score the work.
  • Calibrate first. Both readers score the same three fictional sample files. Talk through any 2 point gap.
  • Look at the whole list. Schools, regions, disciplines, first-timers. If it skews, re-read the band just below the cutline.
Second reader

You read everything. A second reader reads only the band within 8 points of the cutline, plus one in ten at random. A 2 point gap on any criterion = a 10 minute talk, not an average.

Tie-breakers

1 Higher Makes things. 2 Higher Lifts others. 3 First GGP grant over a repeat (repeats are welcome when earned; this only breaks a tie). 4 Both in if the budget allows; if not, you decide and write one line why.

Conflict of interest

Disclose, don’t disappear. A friend, roommate, coworker or mentee: note it, skip scoring that one, the other reader’s score stands. Knowing someone from a GGP event or the Discord is not a conflict; note it and score normally.

Your color code

Green = verified and ready. Orange = can’t verify. Yellow = other issue. Nothing orange gets announced.
