The same world,
twice.

The film itself. The series itself. The show itself. Three worlds that exist twice, watched at the crossing, with three questions for each, then the last hands on your board before it is due.

The film side

A camera chooses where you look. A cut decides what you never see. Someone else built it, and you watch.

The game side

You choose where to look. The loop repeats until you are good at it. Every block is yours.
