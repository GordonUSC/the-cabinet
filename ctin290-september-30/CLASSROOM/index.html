The same world,
twice.

Riot did it. Warner Bros did it to Minecraft. Two studios are doing cinema inside a game engine right now. Three questions for all of them, then the last hands on your board before it is due.

The film side

A camera chooses where you look. A cut decides what you never see. Someone else built it, and you watch.

The game side

You choose where to look. The loop repeats until you are good at it. Every block is yours.
