The three questions

1. What survived the crossing unchanged? 2. What had to be reinvented because the new medium could not carry the original? 3. What is simply gone, and is its absence felt?

Concepts

Adaptation. The crossing of a world from one medium into another. The useful question is never whether it is faithful. It is what survived unchanged, what had to be reinvented because the new medium could not carry the original, and what is simply gone.

What survived. The part of a world that crosses into the new medium unchanged: a name, a color, a silhouette, a rule, a relationship.

What was reinvented. The part that had to be rebuilt because the new medium cannot carry the original form.

What is gone. The part that simply does not come along, and the honest judgment of whether its absence is felt.

In-engine cinematography. Cinema authored inside a game engine: lighting, camera and composition built in the same tool that runs the game.

Finishing pass. A critique that adds nothing and removes what is weakest: count the images, say the palette, name the weakest image and cut it.
