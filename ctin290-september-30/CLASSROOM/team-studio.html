12:15 to 12:45 · The finishing pass

Last hands
on the board.

The file is due tomorrow at five. Nothing new goes on today. Every minute goes into what is already there.

Due
Mood-board file: tomorrow, Thursday October 1, 5 PM Pacific, to Drive, not to Professor Bellamy.
Presentation: Monday, October 5, five minutes, everyone on the team speaks.

Three passes, about seven minutes a team, board on the projector

  1. Pass one
    Count the images out loudThe brief says 20 to 40. A team at 14 has a problem it can still fix tonight. A team at 41 has one to cut.
  2. Pass two
    Say the palette, then check the screenFive colors, by name, said by the team. Then everyone looks at what is actually on screen. Those two things disagree more often than anyone expects.
  3. Pass three
    The weakest image, named by the room, removedNot by the team. Cutting one bad image raises a board faster than adding three good ones, and you will not do it without permission. This is the permission.
  4. Before 12:45
    Write the count, the palette and the cut in My notesThen save your board. Tonight is for the fix pass one found, if any.

Bring today into it

Your game will live in an engine. Does the board show one thing only an engine can do: a camera moving through your space, light that changes as you play, a view no film could hold? One such reference is worth three more pretty stills.
