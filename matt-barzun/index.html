03 / One possible next step

One story.
One small experiment.

A proposal for conversation: take one reported story and test one invitation to participate. Which would be worth trying?

Look before the explanation.

Invite readers to inspect one image or chart, make an observation, then reveal the reporting and its limits. Can they explain what changed in their understanding?

The question: what should a reader be able to do or understand afterward that a headline alone could not offer?

Keep the experiment honest and small

Editorial approval comes first. Separate reported facts from hypothetical choices. Provide a readable non-interactive version. Record production and maintenance effort, accessibility issues and reader misunderstandings before deciding whether to expand. No subscriber-growth claim is implied.
