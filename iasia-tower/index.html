Dr. iAsia's Tower of Excellence

A painted tower of ten floors. On each floor you shoot a basketball through the hoop over a door. Every door you open stays open, and someone climbs up behind you. Each floor is a real chapter of Dr. iAsia Brown's work, with its source, and each one unlocks a one of a kind piece of art for her gallery. Here is every floor, no shots required.

1. The Foundation

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, University of Maryland.

Source: iasiabrown.com/introduction

2. Sixteen Years of Service

Four years in the U.S. Air Force and twelve in the Marine Corps. She rose to senior operational advisor to a Commanding General, across 1.3 million square miles and 1,000+ personnel.

Sources: today.usc.edu (USC News, 2023); lagamecon-edu.com/iasia-brown

3. 126 Million Players

At Microsoft she led the move of Minecraft Realms, 126M+ monthly players in 130+ countries, from AWS to Azure, saving $1.8M+ a year.

Source: iasiabrown.com/introduction

4. Senior Producer

At Xbox Game Studios Publishing she led portfolio strategy and readiness across localization and accessibility, with inclusive design as an operational standard.

Sources: iasiabrown.com/introduction; today.usc.edu (USC News, 2023)

5. Tech Picasso

She picked up a brush in her mid 30s and paints mixed media portraits of people who used their power to uplift others. Colleagues call her Tech Picasso.

Source: today.usc.edu (USC News, 2023)

6. Leave the Door Open

In 2023 she launched the iAsia Brown Endowed Scholarship Fund at the USC School of Cinematic Arts for students in game development and interactive design. In her words: "Funding shouldn't be a barrier."

Sources: her LinkedIn announcement, May 2023; lasentinel.net (2025)

7. The Power Strip

Board member of Women in Technology Hollywood, where she co-chaired its DEI committee, and of Creating Coding Careers. In her words: "I'm not the plug. I'm the power strip."

Sources: withollywood.org; her LinkedIn post, October 2026

8. Outstanding Alumni

Honored by the USC Black Alumni Association as its 2025 Outstanding Alumni Honoree.

Source: blackfilmwire.com (March 2025)

9. Doctor of Education

Doctor of Education in Organizational Change and Leadership, USC Rossier, 2026. Her research: how game designers build social and emotional experiences into games.

Sources: iasiabrown.com/introduction; her LinkedIn posts, April and May 2026

10. Hold the Door

Founder of TechPicasso LLC, investing in early stage game studios; strategic advisor to Kiro'o Games; on the committee for the USC Black Alumni Association's 50th Anniversary.

Sources: iasiabrown.com/introduction; alumni.usc.edu/baa/50th-anniversary
