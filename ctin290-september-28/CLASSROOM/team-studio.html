The last long working block before the file is due Thursday.

Three things, in order
1. Say your palette out loud. Five colors, by name. A team that cannot name them has a collection, not a palette.
2. Show me the one image you cannot justify. Park it.
3. Name what is missing. For a game board it is usually the screen: HUD, camera, split view, a level seen from above.

Also, every Team Project Time

  • Look at the actual board as it stands, not a description of it.
  • Name the single most likely reason this misses Thursday.
  • Agree one thing that will be true by Wednesday, and who does it.

Bring today into it

Does your board show motion, or only mood? One reference that shows how things move, at what rate, with what seam, is worth three more pretty stills.
