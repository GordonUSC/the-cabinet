04

House rules

What keeps a house of AI collaborators trustworthy.

A human says yes

Public launches and messages to other people wait for Gordon's yes.

Two sets of eyes

One agent builds; a different agent checks the facts and tests the result as a reader.

Taste is human

Gordon directs and gives the notes. The AI tools draft art, sound and code; nothing ships until it meets his bar, and it often goes back for another pass.

Private stays private

Friends' details, students' information and anything shared in confidence never leave the room.

05

What we're still figuring out

You have led art and music at scale, so you know this debate better than most. Here is where we have landed so far, honestly.

It drifts toward sameness

Left alone, AI work starts to look alike. Gordon calls it the sameness problem, and his notes are what break it.

It can be confidently wrong

That is why a second agent checks facts against sources, and why tonight's pages went through several rounds of fixes.

Artists' work matters

Creative people are rightly asking hard questions about these tools. We use them to make things for people we care about, and we credit the humans in the room.

We want your take

Where should AI sit in a creative team? What should a student learn to do that no tool will? Gordon would love to hear it.
