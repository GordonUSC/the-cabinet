No sign-in needed to participate

This studio, the examples, the space lab and digital notes open without an account. If your team’s board is unavailable, use digital notes to record your chosen references, palette, one improvement and who does what next. Download those notes before leaving, then apply the decisions to your team’s board when it is available. This page does not upload images or edit a shared board.

A useful place to start

Who needs whom in your game? Which visible choice communicates that? Keep an image, replace one, or change the relationship between references. The goal is a clearer direction, not simply more images.

While you work

  • Make the palette and the intended feeling explicit.
  • Ask why each reference belongs in this particular game.
  • Let every teammate explain or edit a choice.
  • Save the real file and agree who does what next.

These are coaching prompts, not additional graded requirements.
