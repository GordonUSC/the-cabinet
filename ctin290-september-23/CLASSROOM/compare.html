Game and series, change by change

Ten documented changes from The Last of Us game to HBO’s first season. For each one: what survived, what was reinvented, and what is gone, in the creators’ own words where they said it.

Druckmann’s rule for the writers’ room

“A lesser adaptation would be like, let’s take this whole action sequence and build an action episode.” Craig Mazin, on the same room: in the game, relationships “all have to be shown through action because those are the things you’re doing.” What the game does with a fight, the show is allowed to do with a life. That is the rule for every row below.

The Boston quarantine zone

Game chapter / Show episodes 1 and 2

Survived

Oppression built from materials and light: fences and armed men “keeping you from walking anywhere other than where the government wants you to be”; no plants, hard angular materials, a blue overcast “like you’re in a prison.”

Reinvented

Viewpoint. The game shows only what Joel or Ellie sees. The show can “unplug from our main characters and plug into these other characters,” so Marlene and the Fireflies get scenes of their own.

Gone

The optional witness. In the game a family is pulled out and tested on the street, and “you’re given the option to sit and watch it or just walk on by.” Television cannot offer that choice. It chooses for you.

For the room: This is the space lab’s lesson, from the people who built the street: explore, or be shown. What did the show gain by choosing for you, and what did it lose?

Sources: Naughty Dog, Building The Last of Us: Bill’s Town and the Boston QZ (2023).

The opening, and Sarah

Game prologue / Show episode 1

Survived

The birthday watch; the outbreak night; Sarah shot in Joel’s arms, reported as shot for shot from the game. Gabriel Luna: “we’re even kind of framing it in the same way.”

Reinvented

The game puts you inside Sarah, searching the house for her father. Mazin: “We couldn’t give the audience the feeling where you are Sarah.” So the show gives her a whole ordinary day first: breakfast, school, a normal life to lose.

Gone

Playing as Sarah. The years moved from 2013 and 2033 to 2003 and 2023. Added, not in the game: a 1968 talk show that warns about fungus.

For the room: Same scene, two tools. The game makes you the child; the show makes you watch her. Which made you care more, and why?

Sources: Naughty Dog, A Father’s Love (2023-01-18); TV Guide, Megan Vick (2023-01-16); CBR, Katie Doll (2023-02-08); Den of Geek, Matthew Byrd (2023-03-13).

Bill and Frank

Game: Bill’s Town / Show episode 3, “Long, Long Time”

Survived

Bill’s town of traps, its warm sunset palette, “a person’s castle”; his truck and the magazine joke.

Reinvented

In the game Frank is only a body and a letter. The show gives Frank a life and the whole hour to their love story. Druckmann: “A lesser adaptation would be like, let’s take this whole action sequence and build an action episode.” Mazin: in the game, relationships “all have to be shown through action because those are the things you’re doing.”

Gone

The Bill’s Town combat and its bloater, which moves to Kansas City.

For the room: The place is kept and the story inside it is replaced. What do the traps and the sunset still tell you when the plot changes?

Sources: Gizmodo, Germain Lussier (2023-01-30); HBO, Inside the Episode 3; Den of Geek (2023-03-13).

The hospital, and the giraffes

Game ending / Show episode 9, “Look for the Light”

Survived

The giraffes, played “exactly the way it was in the game, as close as we can” (Mazin), with a real giraffe at the Calgary Zoo, not CGI. The hospital, Joel’s choice, the lie, and Ellie’s “okay.”

Reinvented

A flashback to Ellie’s birth and her mother, Anna, explaining her immunity. Joel tells Ellie about his scar.

Gone

The last fight with the infected on the way to the Fireflies.

For the room: The giraffe scene is the purest case of something surviving; the birth flashback is the purest television addition. Put them side by side: what does each do for Ellie?

Sources: Slashfilm, Collider, GamesRadar and GameSpot on the giraffe (2023-03); Den of Geek (2023-03-13).

Six more changes

Infection: spores become tendrils

Game throughout / Show episode 2

Survived

Clickers stay the fear object. Druckmann: “We were always wary of how we differentiate ourselves from zombies.”

Reinvented

Action. Mazin: “We can’t crouch, bandage, and be fine.” A fight the game would rarely put in a cutscene becomes a story scene.

Gone

Airborne spores and gas masks. The show spreads infection by touch and a fungal network, so faces are never hidden behind masks.

Sources: Naughty Dog, Blooming Terror (2023-01-24); Den of Geek.

Tess

Game chapter / Show episode 2

Survived

Her last words: take Ellie to the Fireflies.

Reinvented

Joel and Tess become a couple; she dies to the infected, not to soldiers.

Gone

Her death by gunfire.

Sources: CBR; Den of Geek.

Pittsburgh becomes Kansas City

Game / Show episodes 4 and 5

Survived

The hunters’ ambush, almost word for word and shot for shot.

Reinvented

Kathleen, who is not in the game; Henry as an informant; Sam is deaf.

Gone

Ellie’s own first kill; the steady stream of infected in the tunnels.

Sources: CBR; Den of Geek.

Jackson

Game / Show episode 6

Survived

Tommy and the town.

Reinvented

Joel’s panic attacks shown on screen; he says his fear out loud.

Gone

Ellie running away after she overhears.

Sources: Den of Geek.

Left Behind

Game DLC / Show episode 7

Survived

Riley, the mall, the arcade night.

Reinvented

The girls really play Mortal Kombat II instead of Ellie imagining a game.

Gone

The raider-filled supply run.

Sources: Den of Geek.

David

Game winter / Show episode 8

Survived

The danger David poses to Ellie.

Reinvented

David as a preacher with far more screen time; Ellie ends it herself.

Gone

The infected sequence with David.

Sources: Den of Geek.
