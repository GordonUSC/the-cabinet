One World,
Two Media

Individual video essay · 3-5 minutes
Due Thursday September 24, 5 PM Pacific

Choose one world that exists in two forms. Show both, speak over both, and compare what survived, what had to be reinvented and what is gone. Production value is not assessed; visual observation and the comparative argument are.

Approved course pairs

  • The Last of Us game and series
  • League of Legends and Arcane
  • Minecraft and the film
  • Hamilton on stage and on screen

A different pair needs approval in the course Discord. Do not change your pair simply because today's example is different.

The two rubric criteria

Visual Observation · 5 points

Specific camera work, composition, color, lighting and movement, precisely observed and annotated on screen.

Comparative Argument · 5 points

What survived, what was reinvented and what is gone, supported by specific evidence; course terms used accurately. The claim about absence must be argued.

Full established assignment and all rubric bands
