Shannon Murray is our host for Palm Springs, and one of my dearest friends from my San Francisco years. This weekend belongs to that long friendship as much as it belongs to the music.

MONDAY · NOVEMBER 9And, of course, my birthday.

Pride fills November 6 to 8. My birthday is Monday, November 9. The parade and the birthday are different days this year, and both belong in our November 6 to 9 chapter.

The official Pride dates ↗

THE BEGINNINGA ballroom before the whole city.

Palm Springs Pride began in 1986 as “Sizzle,” an evening of dinner and local entertainers in a hotel ballroom. The scale has changed; the reason to gather remains visible in that small beginning: a community making space for itself.

Read Palm Springs Pride’s history ↗

THE LIVING TRADITIONJoy carries a history.

The organization places its celebration in the legacy of Stonewall and equal rights. The parade and festival are ways to be visible together. Here, the invitation is simple: there is room for the version of you that feels most like you.
