Dig into the sound and story

5 short chapters. Open the ones that call to you.

THE COLLABORATIONTwo names. A third personality.

Dog Blood is Skrillex and Boys Noize together. Boys Noize’s own discography traces Middle Finger to 2012 and Middle Finger Pt. 2 to 2013. Follow those records into Turn Off the Lights and listen for the personality that emerges when the two artists meet.

Dig through Boys Noize’s official discography ↗

THE DEEPER CUTFour doors into Turn Off the Lights.

The official EP page sets out four tracks: “BREAK LAW,” “4 MIND” with josh pan and X&G, “KOKOE” with Otira, and “TURN OFF THE LIGHTS.” Follow the featured collaborators as another route out of a song you love. This is catalog exploration, not a prediction of Portola’s set.

Explore the EP and official videos ↗

OUR HISTORYBefore this waterfront, another crowd.

At HARD Summer on August 6, 2023, Brennan helped me through the crowd for Skrillex and Four Tet. At NiteHarts on October 11, 2025, we found each other again from different parts of the field. Portola adds another chance to turn around and see him there.

MY LONGER THREADThis story goes back to 2015.

Skrillex and Boys Noize at the Game Awards afterparty in 2015. Skrillex’s EDC surprise in May 2019. Dog Blood at Electric Zoo later that year, reconstructed from my own account. A TroyBoi birthday surprise at the Shrine that November. Second Sky in 2022; Red Rocks, Los Angeles in June, and HARD Summer in 2023; NiteHarts in 2025. Nine nights in my checked archive. Portola is the next page, not the beginning.

THE CHOICELet an ending count.

A festival evening is an edit. The published timetable makes the Dog Blood and Soulwax overlap real, so we can choose our ending instead of chasing an impossible plan. An earlier Despacio detour is another route through the day.
