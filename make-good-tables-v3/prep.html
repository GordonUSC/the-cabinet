Make Good Tables · Center for Public Leadership · draft run card

The people in the room are the material.

A working draft for an afternoon with about 30 people, built only from the workshop proposal: the anonymous survey first, then three parts, then the fourth seat as the thing they carry out.

Timings assume a three-hour afternoon with one break. They are a starting point to cut, stretch or throw out. Nothing here is booked or confirmed.

Before the room: the anonymous survey

Short and anonymous, sent a few days ahead, so the afternoon can open with what the group already said. Drawn from the four themes in the proposal.

  1. Think of a time another person changed your thinking. What did they do that made it possible?
  2. In a group, what makes you speak up? What makes you hold back?
  3. When someone disagrees with you at a table, what do you usually do first?
  4. Describe the best group you have been part of in one sentence.
  5. Describe the worst one in one sentence.
  6. What did the best one do that the worst one never did?

Run of show

0:00 to 0:15 · OPEN

What you told us

Open with the survey patterns, not a definition. Read three answers aloud from the best-group and worst-group questions, then open live anonymous questions to surface what is usually hidden.

0:15 to 0:55 · PART 1

What are we doing here?

  • Name the models people carry in: exchange views, reach a decision, win the room.
  • Introduce the fourth seat: it is always at the table, and it stays empty until everyone puts something in.
  • Show the same meeting played three tempting ways (win, give in, split the difference) in the guide, and let the room watch the seat stay empty.

0:55 to 1:35 · PART 2

What do we do with difference?

  • Difference as raw material: evolution, discovery, invention, the common law, art.
  • Ask the room which they do most: make too much of it, too little, or avoid it.
  • Pairs: each person names one thing the other brought that they could not have brought themselves.

1:35 to 1:50 · BREAK

Shuffle the tables during the break so Part 3 starts in new groups.

1:50 to 2:45 · PART 3

How do we make a good table?

  • Small, changing groups of three, each with an empty fourth chair at the table.
  • Each group gets a small real task. Round one, each person tries to get exactly what they brought. Round two, they try to make something none of them would make alone.
  • Debrief one question: what is sitting in the fourth chair now?
  • Hand out the navigation tool: recognize the pattern, name it, change course.

2:45 to 3:00 · CLOSE

One table you will change

Each person writes one table they share, at HKS, at work or at home, and one thing they will put in its fourth seat.

The fourth seat, four ways

The one image to carry through every part.

WinEmpty. One person leaves with exactly what they brought; the other two leave with nothing.
Give inEmpty. One contribution never reaches the table.
Split the differenceEmpty. Two people each keep half of what they brought.
Make somethingFilled. Something is there that no one could have made alone.
