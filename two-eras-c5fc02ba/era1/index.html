Age 6

Pele at RFK

Tap the ball. Take the shot.

Pele and the Cosmos at RFK Stadium. Nothing has topped it.

"nothing yet has topped the thrill of going to see Pele at RFK stadium when I was six years old... the Cosmos were magic"Gordon on BigSoccer, March 30, 2006
"I kicked my first ball at age 4, wearing #4 on the Reston Rattlers"Gordon on Instagram, April 7, 2026

His scrapbook holds a Rattlers "Champs '76" certificate, position: wing.

Age 5

The mutant

Freeze them with your mind.

Freezing did not work. He picked a different power.

"at first I tried to freeze things with my mind we didn't work out like I thought"Gordon, award acceptance remarks, uploaded March 23, 2018
He chose Rogue, "because dealing with me can be a little draining and I like to take on the best traits of my friends"Same remarks, 2018

Snow on the gridiron

"I had four sets of home and away cowboys... We'd play snow games where I put snow on my electric board, wind games where I turn the fan on"Gordon, Playmakers podcast ep. 12, July 14, 2017

1984

The team, then the podium

Fill the four seats.

May 1984: Virginia's four-person team wins the first MATHCOUNTS national title.

November 14, 1984. Five days after turning 14, he speaks at the U.S. Department of Education.

"I have the ability to think clearly, to create, to contribute, to encourage others"Gordon, age 14, his remarks at the Department of Education, November 14, 1984 (scan returned by dot)
"It is so interesting to me to hear how I synthesized things back then and how I talk about the importance of trajectory now"Gordon on Instagram, April 17, 2019

To 1988

Ten schools

Stamp every door.

Ten schools. Three high schools. The last one felt like his.

"10 schools growing up. I went to three high schools. So shout out, Exeter and St. Paul's"Gordon, Valued Cultures, July 9, 2025
"It was a meaningful time for me, to feel valued and celebrated for who I am. I had the freedom to follow my own path"Gordon, St. Paul's School alumni spotlight, Class of 1988

Exeter, 1985: where he met Foster Birch, a friend he would later bring to EA.

  • +1 TEAM
  • ITEM: St. Paul's, Class of 1988
Summer 1987

The magical summer

Connect the stars.

The Research Science Institute at MIT. A new stat appears. Nobody earns it. Someone gives it.

"just being enough as we were right and it was like the last sort of magical summer"Gordon, CEE STEM Lyceums talk, uploaded October 20, 2025

Class of 1993

Two yeses, a water plant, a borrowed Genesis

Open an envelope.

Harvard and Stanford both said yes. He chose Harvard.

Admitted to the Class of 1992. He deferred one year and graduated with the Class of 1993.

Then an engineering field trip. He knew it was not him.

"i learned two very important things that day one that i hold these people in the highest regard who we trust with our drinking water ... the second was that i'm not one of those people"Gordon, XPRIZE Connect, uploaded November 5, 2020
To a friend: "for letting me play your Sega Genesis... It turned into a career"Gordon, Facebook group post, April 29, 2013
  • +1 MIND
  • +1 PLAY
  • ITEM: The borrowed Genesis
Spring 1993

Click. Click. Click.

Call everyone in the credits.

ESPN and EA passed. So he called every name in the NHL Hockey credits.

"Hi, I'm Gordon. Click. Hi, I'm an engineer from Harvard. Click. Hi, I really love your game. Click. Hi, please let me work with you. Click"Gordon, Playmakers ep. 12, July 14, 2017
Down in the special thanks, "a guy named Jim Simmons... turned out to be the wrong Jim Simmons, but the right one for me"Gordon, GamesBeat, 2020
  • +1 VOICE
  • ITEM: The right wrong Jim Simmons
1993

The tester test

Call the play. Then fax the league.

One hour, one yellow pad, Bulls vs. Blazers. He started at EA in June 1993.

"a yellow legal pad eight and a half by 14" ... "it all played out like you would imagine in a in a montage from a film"Gordon, Bakersfield College talks, 2021

In 2017 he says "Bulls versus Blazers or sorry, Bulls versus Lakers" (Playmakers). Both are his words.

"when i arrived at ea at day one for orientation i had my madden binder ready to go"Gordon, Bakersfield College, 2021. He had faxed every NFL team from his dorm for rosters.
He slept "under the desks" and sat "in the offices of people whose job I wanted to have in the future and visualize myself there"Gordon, Microsoft GDC fireside, 2018
  • +1 MAKER
  • +1 TEAM
  • ITEM: The Madden binder
1994

Set the default

One skin tone for all. Choose.

Madden NFL 95. Most NFL players were Black. He made Black the default.

"once I got in the room where it happens, I made the bold choice to make African American players default... for once I wasn't doing the mental gymnastics"Gordon, Lead With Your Brand, February 9, 2021
"we also introduced create a player in that era because having representation is so important to people"Gordon, Black Game Pros Sessions, February 2026
"back in 95 ... we put our actual personal emails in the manual"Gordon, CMX Summit, 2020

Launch day, November 18, 1994. The co-designer asks the players.

"now the game is out and I am eager to talk in depth"Gordon, rec.sport.football.pro, November 18, 1994
"they emphasized the importance of passing lanes" ... "tapping the button increases your chances of throwing over a defender"Gordon answering a player, alt.sega.genesis, January 18, 1995

Found by dot; checked at the original Usenet posts.

Nov 1995

Z-Axis, and a Monday

Stock the studio shelf.

He left EA for Z-Axis, a studio started by his St. Paul's friend Dave Luntz.

"I came over to Z-Axis in November of 1995."Gordon, PSExtreme #12, November 1999, pp. 46 to 47 (also in his scrapbook)

Someone gave him his shot. Here he gives one.

"the design lead that hired me and gave me my first job"Alan Blaine on Gordon at Z-Axis, Out of Play Area ep. 28, March 2022, 1:46:46. Alan went on to design Thrasher.
Next Generation fakes a jump shot in a two-player demo and "suckered the creative director into leaping too early"Next Generation #36, December 1997, p. 138
"I was an original Z-Axis creative director"Gordon, text, February 27, 2019

Fall 1997, after a weekend in Los Angeles with his friend Ted: "I came out at work that Monday."

His words, Facebook, January 27, 2019. Coming out stage one: at work.

"from just sort of I, as a achiever, to we as collaborator, team member, community member"Gordon on EA and San Francisco, Valued Cultures, July 9, 2025

2.8.98

Sunday Funday at The Café

Bring them together.

Sunday, February 8, 1998. Gordon was out with Ted. Joe Heally was out with Jeff. Their song became "Together Again."

"we met 2/8/1998 on a Sunday Funday"Gordon, November 26, 2026

Together since then: 28 years, 9 months

One month in: "we NEED to go to E3 Expo... in Atlanta... I also have a gay soccer tournament with the San Francisco Spikes Soccer Club that weekend in Atlanta"Gordon on Instagram, February 9, 2021, remembering 1998

Game week and a queer soccer weekend in one trip. Years later he named that crossing PridE3.

  • +1 TEAM
  • ITEM: "Together Again"
2001 to 2007

Two more doors

Fill the plus-one. Then make the cut.

THQ holiday party, about December 2001. He brought Joe.

"You have the plus one ... I did not want Joe to experience the feelings that were inside me. So I invited him. ... I felt accountable. So we did."Gordon, Microsoft GDC fireside, 2018

The order matters in his telling: the Z-Axis sale "gave us some padding, to live our lives, to be a little more free."

At Spike TV a designer, Jeb Havens, feared a segment misrepresented him. Gordon pushed for the edit.

"That was really the coming out because it was like 'Okay. Like our truth is more important than the money. Let's just see how it goes.'"Gordon, Microsoft GDC fireside, 2018
"coming out is a lifelong process"Same fireside

  • +1 VOICE
  • ITEM: The plus-one
About 2007

The back patio

Bring someone in.

An anonymous mailing list became a night at the Lone Star. That grew into GGP.

"What if we met outside these closed doors." ... "people just tiptoeing back there like, 'Do you like games? I like games.'"Gordon, Microsoft GDC fireside, 2018
"It's so that young, queer people won't ever know what it's like to not have a party"Same fireside

Brian Rubin-Sowers passed a paper sign-up sheet around at GDC 2006 and later "passed the reins over to Gordon." GGP, co-founded about 2006 with Brian.
