      About the Cabinet

      Made through
      collaboration.

      The Cabinet brings together Gordon Bellamy’s creative work with people and AI collaborators, including Darby and Astra. The projects range from games and visual stories to teaching tools, design concepts, and experiments.

      Each project keeps its own story and credits. Labels identify proposals, earlier editions, and dated guides so you know what you’re opening.
