Moss & Mineral

Pine corduroy, chalk mesh and a small woven trail badge. Quiet, useful, built around the landscape.

Palette Pine #294739 · Sage #A6B39A · Chalk #F2EFE2 · Ochre #C49B45 · Navy accent #23394C

  • Cap forest-pine fine corduroy front and bill, ivory mesh back, small rectangular cream woven GRYPHUS badge with a bird, navy rear snap and tiny navy woven side tab
  • Bag pine compact bicycle frame bag with sage geometric contour print and navy zipper tape
  • Bottle ivory squeeze cycling bottle with ochre cap and fine pine contours
  • Socks cream cycling socks with pine cuff and ochre slim stripe

Poster A graphic trail-map composition: large ochre sun, layered sage and pine contour lines, a tiny soaring black bird. Cream margin, assertive condensed GRYPHUS across top. Sophisticated European outdoor print, not camouflage.

Canyon Current

Clay corduroy meets sand mesh. A flash of turquoise takes the trail into festival color.

Palette Clay #B75E43 · Sand #EDDCBC · Rust #7C3E2F · Turquoise #4C9694 · Navy accent #23394C

  • Cap terracotta fine corduroy front, dark rust curved bill, sand mesh rear, small navy rectangular embroidered GRYPHUS wordmark badge with thin turquoise border; clean uninterrupted crown-to-bill seam
  • Bag terracotta bicycle frame bag with turquoise zipper and abstract rust terrain stripe
  • Bottle sand cycling squeeze bottle with turquoise cap and rust bird silhouette
  • Socks sand cycling socks with terracotta cuff, small turquoise block

Poster Bold risograph poster of abstract Texas limestone escarpments, terracotta, sand and turquoise broad horizontal torn-paper forms, a rust sun, small soaring bird, huge cream GRYPHUS in elegant condensed letters. Strong diagonal singletrack line winds through. Four-color handmade outdoor art print.

Meadow Bloom

Moss and oat make room for lilac and sun yellow. A playful, expressive outdoor collection.

Palette Moss #52684A · Lilac #B9ADD0 · Lemon #EBCB53 · Oat #F0EADC · Navy accent #23394C

  • Cap moss-green fine corduroy front, moss curved bill, oat mesh back, small lilac oval embroidered patch containing dark moss GRYPHUS and a small yellow sun, navy rear snap; clean brim seam
  • Bag moss bicycle frame bag with restrained lilac large curved graphic and navy zipper
  • Bottle lilac squeeze cycling bottle with moss cap and lemon sun/bird icon
  • Socks oat cycling socks with moss cuff and lilac/yellow block details

Poster Playful sophisticated silkscreen poster: large lemon-yellow sun behind an abstract moss-green hill, lilac-blue negative sky, oversized typographic GRYPHUS in dark moss following straight strong baseline, graphic wildflower petals small at bottom, small soaring bird. Outdoor record-sleeve energy, no alpine snow or invented geographic landmarks. Strong restrained compositions, not cute clipart.

River Signal

Teal, reed and limestone with a precise navy edge. A fresh technical take on the weekend.

Palette Deep teal #285C5A · Reed #C4A95F · Limestone #EFE7D6 · Clay #BC7355 · Navy accent #23394C

  • Cap reed-khaki fine corduroy crown front, deep teal curved bill, limestone mesh back, small deep teal embroidered GRYPHUS lettering, a tiny navy woven side label, absolutely no cord trim
  • Bag deep teal bicycle frame bag with ochre diagonal panel and navy zipper tape
  • Bottle limestone cycling squeeze bottle with teal cap and ochre river-like bands
  • Socks cream cycling socks with deep teal cuff and one ochre wave band

Poster Swiss-modern outdoor festival print: deep teal ground, rhythmic large cream and ochre flowing river curves becoming musical grooves, a terracotta sun, precisely set uppercase cream GRYPHUS at top. Small soaring bird in sky. Flat bold screenprinted design with richly tactile matte paper, minimal copy, not a busy map.

Golden Hour

Sun-washed ochre, canvas and evergreen. Familiar camping colors with a warm soul-record spirit.

Palette Ochre #C5923D · Faded orange #CB764A · Evergreen #304C3C · Canvas #F1E4CB · Navy accent #23394C

  • Cap warm ochre fine corduroy front and curved bill, canvas-colored mesh back, small evergreen shield-shaped patch with canvas GRYPHUS and sunrise graphic, subtle navy side tab and rear snap; plain clean bill seam
  • Bag evergreen bicycle frame bag with ochre diagonal side panel and navy zipper
  • Bottle ochre squeeze cycling bottle with evergreen cap and small canvas sun-and-wing icon
  • Socks canvas cycling socks with evergreen cuff and faded-orange narrow stripes

Poster Vivid elegant vintage outdoor woodcut/soul-record poster, evergreen silhouettes of rolling Hill Country and live oaks, wide faded-orange and golden ochre sunset radiating bands, cream sun and small black soaring bird. Very large expressive condensed dark evergreen GRYPHUS set across cream upper area, warm screenprinting. No mountains from another region.

Supplier rule No ropes, cords, braids or decorative piping across any hat's crown-to-bill seam. Plain seam only.
