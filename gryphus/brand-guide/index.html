CREATIVE STANDARDS10 / 22

Make partnership
feel intentional.

600 × 400 mm / DIRECTION MUST BE VERIFIED ON SITE

Gryphus leads

Separate partner artwork from editorial content. Align partners by optical height; start at a maximum 0.7 × Gryphus cap height and honor their clear-space requirements.

Presenting-tier treatment needs specific organizer approval. Never invent a sponsor relationship.

One sign. One decision.

One destination and one clear arrow. Begin with 80 mm cap height for distance reading; test on site. Confirm fixing, material, weather and accessibility with the venue/vendor.

LOGO REPRODUCTION / VENDOR STARTING POINTS22 / 22

Small marks.
High standards.

A working vector system

Nine SVGs: color symbol, one-color symbol and outlined-wordmark lockup for each option. No external fonts or raster. Keep proportions and at least one-quarter symbol-width clear space. Begin proofing at 24 mm print or 40 mm embroidery; these are sample sizes, not guaranteed minima.

The product decides the detail

Test the smallest use at actual size. Keep counters and wing gaps open in stitch or weave. Approve thread, print and material samples. All hats have a plain brim seam: no ropes, cords, braids or decorative piping. Preserve editable originals and the unmodified vector masters.

The low Hill Country is the actual place reference. Heritage Sun’s triangle is a symbolic heritage cue, not a depiction of the ranch. None of these marks is a final approved brand. The earlier generated emblem is reference material.
