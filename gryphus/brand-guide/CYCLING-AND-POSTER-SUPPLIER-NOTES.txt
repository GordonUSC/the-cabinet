GRYPHUS / NEW OUTDOOR COLLECTIONS / DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

These are visual directions for review. No manufacturer, SKU, price, order or safety performance is asserted.

HATS
Every option uses a corduroy-front, mesh-back trucker. No rope, braid, cord or decorative piping at the brim. Plain construction seam only. See HAT-SUPPLIER-REQUIREMENTS.txt.

FRAME BAGS
Match the selected palette and artwork placement; navy is a small zipper/hardware accent. Obtain a supplier drawing and actual sample. Confirm mounting system, frame dimensions, knee clearance, cable routing, access to bottle cages and abrasion protection. Images are styling studies, not cutting patterns. No waterproof or capacity rating has been verified.

BOTTLES
Squeeze cycling bottle is the proposed form. Confirm actual capacity, cage fit, closure, food-contact documentation, wash care and decoration compatibility with the chosen supplier. Obtain the supplier's printable-area drawing. No safety certification is claimed by the visual concept.

SOCKS
Confirm sizing, fiber composition, knit construction, shrinkage, care and the achievable detail of a woven or knit mark. Limit navy to a small accent. Do not translate a detailed raster bird directly into a small knit mark.

POSTERS
Starting format: 18 x 24 inches (457.2 x 609.6 mm). Confirm trim size, 3 mm bleed, stock, color profile and finishing with the printer. Keep live copy at least 12 mm inside trim. A screen-print treatment is a direction; separations and number of inks are not settled. The generated photographed posters demonstrate composition, not press-ready art. Commission/export clean masters after a direction and logo are selected; do not upscale a poster cropped from a product mockup and call it print-ready.

PLACE + EVENT COPY
Use low Texas Hill Country terrain, limestone, caliche, live oak and cedar. No tall/alpine mountains. Abstract terrain and river-like poster curves are artwork, not a map or claim about ranch amenities.
