The HTML links back to the integrated website when hosted at /gryphus/brand-guide/. It remains readable offline; Switzer may fall back to Arial unless installed locally. The PDF embeds its typography.

Choose one design direction per campaign. Proposed standards do not authorize a production run.

logos/: three concept directions, each with a scalable symbol, one-color symbol and outlined wordmark lockup. Nine native SVGs; no final selection is assumed.
wordmarks/: six SVG files with LIVE EDITABLE TEXT, not outlined paths. Install fonts/BarlowCondensed-Black.ttf before editing. Convert a copy to outlines in your design application after approval. Preserve editable originals.
templates/: flat t-shirt placements, cap and patch artwork areas, signage and partner spacing. They are not manufacturing patterns, product photographs or final print separations. Check dimensions and processes with the vendor.
color-tokens.json: exact digital palettes and unprofiled CMYK starting points; no Pantone match.
licenses/: Barlow OFL and font/asset notes. Obtain Switzer and Georgia through their original licensed sources. Those source binaries are not in this kit.
look-specifications.json: five coordinated outdoor looks with exact palettes and product styling notes. New look choices are independent of website direction and earlier merchandise collection choices.
assets/: campaign concepts and documentary references. Generated art does not establish actual venue, performer, guest or program. Cale/Gordon photograph: April 24, 2026; photographer credit unverified. Reconfirm personal-image reproduction on goods.

MANDATORY HAT RULE: No ropes on any hats. No braids, cords or decorative band above the brim. Current options use corduroy-front / mesh-back trucker construction. See HAT-SUPPLIER-REQUIREMENTS.txt.

2027 NIGHT DIRECTION: Live music until 10 pm; a secret DJ concept until midnight. Disko Cowboy is listening inspiration, not a confirmed booking. After-dark styling belongs to this optional moment, not the full outdoor brand. Actual historical event schedules are unchanged.

PLACE RULE: low Texas Hill Country limestone, caliche, live oak and cedar. Do not substitute tall or alpine mountains. Posters may use abstract graphic terrain, never imply an accurate venue map.
