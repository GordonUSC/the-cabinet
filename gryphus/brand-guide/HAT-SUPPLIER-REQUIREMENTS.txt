GRYPHUS / HAT SUPPLIER REQUIREMENTS / SEPTEMBER 2026

MANDATORY BRAND RULE: NO ROPES ON ANY HATS.

Do not add a rope, braid, cord, decorative band or decorative piping across the base of the crown or above the brim. A standard construction seam is not a decorative rope. Reject samples that add one, including samples based on older concept images.

All current style options use a trucker construction: corduroy front panels are welcome; the rear is visibly breathable mesh. Corduroy must read as fabric texture, not as an applied cord. Use the selected outdoor palette. Navy is a small supporting accent, not the dominant cap color.

Artwork notes:
- Use the approved GRYPHUS wordmark; bird artwork needs an approved production master.
- Start with a 90 x 40 mm front artwork area, then use the supplier's actual safe embroidery area and avoid the center seam.
- Keep embroidery simple enough to preserve letter counters and the approved mark at actual size. Confirm thread, backing and stitch density on a physical sample.
- Do not treat generated imagery as a selected manufacturer, material specification, proof or order.

Required sample views: front, side and rear, so the no-rope construction, corduroy texture and mesh panels can all be checked.

Before production: confirm the chosen direction, final artwork, garment construction, dimensions, material, color sample and physical proof with the organizer. This guide does not authorize spending or an order.
