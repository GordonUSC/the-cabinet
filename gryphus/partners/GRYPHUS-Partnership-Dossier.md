# GRYPHUS / Partnership dossier for Cale

Research checked September 20, 2026. All entries are prospects; no outreach, agreement, booking, exclusivity or budget has been committed.

## Start here

1. **Own the trails:** Hill Country Bicycle Works + Skratch Labs.
2. **Make rest real:** Soulbird Wellness + Hyperice.
3. **Invite people in:** Visit Comfort TX + Athletic Brewing Co.

## How to use the tiers

- **Tier 1:** Direct audience fit, useful guest service or clear local connection.
- **Tier 2:** Build out the program once the operating plan is defined.
- **Tier 3:** Niche, complex or unresolved prospects.

## Scope and method

The [Runningman homepage](https://runningmanfestival.com/) displayed 60 partner-logo positions at the bottom of the page: 59 unique brands, with Lone River appearing twice. Visual logo inspection corrected inaccurate alt text. The source assets include a 2025 upload path, so this documents the wall as displayed on September 20, 2026, not an exclusive 2026 contract roster. Each brand has its own source and original logo link below.

Twelve San Antonio / Hill Country prospects and one user-picked music / merchandise prospect are separately identified. Activations and priorities are recommendations. Public general-support routes are not presented as direct sponsorship contacts. KOH, Collective Water and I Believe In You remain unresolved business identities; ask Runningman rather than contacting guessed businesses.

## First outreach brief

Include confirmed date, audience and site plan; one guest experience; cash/product/staff/equipment/co-promotion requested; proposed deliverables and measurement. Clarify freight, staff, power, water, sanitation, access, insurance and weather responsibilities. Do not promise unapproved inclusions.
