What Cale should send.

A short invitation with the confirmed date, audience and site plan; one proposed guest experience; the specific support requested; and how the partner’s contribution will be measured.

  • Ask: cash, product, staff, equipment or co-promotion. Say which.
  • Offer: a defined space, useful guest experience, agreed content and a simple results report.
  • Confirm: freight, staff, power, water, sanitation, access, insurance and weather responsibilities.

Ideas here are recommendations, not promised inclusions. No outreach has been sent and no budget or exclusivity has been agreed.
