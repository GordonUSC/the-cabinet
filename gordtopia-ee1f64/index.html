The big picture, 60 seconds

Seven circles of life, each feeding the next

Every loop takes something in and hands something on. The dots show what flows where. Pick a loop to see what it feeds, or tap a circle to jump to its page.

How to read it

Start at 1

Drive the loops in order. Food first, then compost, animals, villagers, iron, trade and gear. The gear loop buys beds, which grow the village and the iron loop again.

×2

The rule that matters most: each villager buys at most its stock twice a day. Your emeralds are capped by how many villagers you have, not how big your farms are. Grow the village.

Loops 1 & 2, Soil and compost

The Fields

Eighteen crops, how long they really take on Bedrock, and how to turn every leftover seed into bone meal.

Try it, rows vs a solid block

Same seeds, same water, twice the speed

Rows: about 72 min to ripen
Solid block: about 143 min

A crop grows slower when the same crop sits diagonally next to it or on both sides. Alternate wheat with carrots or potatoes and keep water within 4 blocks.

The Trading Hall

Where crops, animals and iron become emeralds, and emeralds become Mending, pearls, saddles and beds.

Your question, how to tell farmers apart

Hold the crop. Watch his hands.

On Bedrock every farmer wears the same straw hat, and his level badge only shows his level. But hold a crop near him: if he buys it, he raises his arms and holds up an emerald. Hold an emerald and he shows what he sells. It doesn't work in the evening, at night, or when that trade is sold out.

What a farmer plants is unrelated to what he buys. He plants whatever seeds he picked up first, from 8 inventory slots you can't see.

This farmer buys only one of four crops. Which one?

Set up the hall once

  1. Roll. Put a new farmer in his stall. Hold the crop you want. No emerald? Break and replace his composter, then hold it again.
  2. Watch for thieves. On Bedrock any jobless villager with a bed, anywhere in the village, can claim the new composter. Reroll when no spare villagers are around.
  3. Lock. Make one trade. His offers are now fixed forever.
  4. Name him. Name tags now craft from 1 paper and any 1 nugget: iron, gold or copper. "Carrot Carl" never despawns.
  5. Label the stall. An item frame with his crop above the stall, and a wool strip in that crop's color.
  6. Group the rows. The carrot row, the pumpkin row. The boys walk straight to the right buyer.

The mining kit, check before you go

The Nether, ancient debris

Netherite lives at Y 8 to 23

On Bedrock, ancient debris is spread evenly from Y 8 to Y 23, with rarer pockets up to Y 119. Y 15 is a good middle. It never touches air, so you only find it by tunneling. It needs a diamond pickaxe and shrugs off explosions.

  1. Smelt each debris into netherite scrap.
  2. Combine 4 scrap and 4 gold ingots into 1 netherite ingot.
  3. Upgrade diamond gear at a smithing table with a netherite upgrade template from a bastion. Copy a template with 7 diamonds and 1 netherrack.

Bring fire resistance potions. The lava sea's surface is at Y 31, so your tunnels run below it: never break a block above your head without a plan.

Where the debris is

Y 15Lava sea Y 31

On Bedrock every chunk gets two debris pockets between Y 8 and 23, plus three more anywhere up to Y 119. That's more debris than Java gets. Tunnel at Y 15, under the lava sea. Debris never touches air, so you only find it by digging, and it shrugs off explosions.

Who's who, tap a mob

Ghast

Fireballs blow up most blocks, but not cobblestone. Hit a fireball back with your sword to send it home.

Piglin

Wear one piece of gold armor and they leave you alone. Don't open chests or mine gold near them.

Piglin brute

Gold doesn't calm brutes. Guards in bastions, always hostile. Fight with a shield up.

Hoglin

Charges and throws you. Backs away from warped fungus, portals and respawn anchors.

Zombified piglin

Neutral until you hit one. Then the whole group comes for you.

Blaze

Fortress only. Shoots fireballs, so drink fire resistance. Snowballs hurt them.

Wither skeleton

Fortress only. Its hits wither you. It is too tall for a tunnel 2 blocks high, so dig one and hide.

Magma cube

Splits into smaller cubes when hit. Drops magma cream for fire resistance potions.

Strider

Friendly. Saddle it and steer with warped fungus on a stick to cross the lava sea.

Bring one home

Grow a happy ghast

Dried ghasts sit next to bone fossils in soul sand valleys, and piglins sometimes trade one. Put it in water at home. In about 20 minutes it wakes up as a baby ghastling, and when it grows up it's a happy ghast: put a harness on it and up to four of you can fly together.

  1. Find a dried ghast by a fossil or from bartering.
  2. Soak it: place it in water, or water on it.
  3. Wait about 20 minutes for the ghastling.
  4. Harness the grown happy ghast and fly.

Nether rules, they're different here

  • Beds explodeSleeping in the Nether sets off a blast bigger than TNT. Set your spawn with a respawn anchor instead: 6 crying obsidian and 3 glowstone.
  • Water boils awayBuckets of water vanish in steam. Bring blocks and potions, not water.
  • Compasses and clocks spinWrite coordinates down, or link a compass to a lodestone.
  • Lava runs fastNether lava flows 8 blocks, three times faster than at home. Never dig straight up or down.
  • A roof of bedrockThe ceiling is bedrock from Y 123 to 127. The world is 128 blocks tall.
  • Eight to oneOne block walked here is eight blocks at home. That's what makes it a highway.
  • Home stands stillCrops, golems and villager restocks only run near a player. While everyone is in the Nether, the farms at home pause. On long trips, leave one brother on station.
