Nobody expects your prototype to be working today. Broken, half-built and rough are all fine. We want to see where you are and where you're headed.

Your ten minutes

  • About six minutes from your team. All four of you speak, so decide before 10:10 who covers what.
  • End on your goal for the next meeting.
  • Then about four minutes from the room.

How we give feedback

I like…Something that's working, and why it works.
What if…One thing to try next.
I can help…A skill or trick your team could lend them.

Talk about the work, be specific, and keep it kind.

Ideas for your work time

  • Re-stage the shot in the engine, or move the camera.
  • Re-light it: lights, materials, post-process.
  • Adjust shaders, particles or timing.
  • Re-render, or re-cut the sequence.
