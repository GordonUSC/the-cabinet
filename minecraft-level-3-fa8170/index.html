Level 3 slot rolls, what to accept

The top slot showing 3 costs 1 XP level + 1 lapis and consumes 3 XP worth from your bar. These are the odds for the primary enchantment you will see revealed, computed from the Bedrock formula, for each item type.

The one rule

Level 3 is a cheap scouting roll. The useful rolls are Efficiency I on mining tools, Sharpness I or Protection I on gear, and Power I on bows. Anything else, break a block to burn the roll and try again.

You cannot get Fortune, Silk Touch, Looting, Infinity, Mending or Unbreaking III at level 3. Those need the bottom slot (and 15 bookshelves).

Why the odds look the way they do. At xpCost = 3 the internal enchantment cost (eCost) lands around 3 to 9 for diamond/iron/netherite. That window is low enough that only Level I variants of a handful of enchantments qualify. Selection is weighted: common (weight 10) beats uncommon (5) beats rare (2) beats very rare (1). Fortune, Silk Touch, Looting, Infinity and Channeling all have Level I cost minimums of 15 to 25, way above this window.
