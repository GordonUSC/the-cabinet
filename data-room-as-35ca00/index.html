The one-minute summary

GGP is a nonprofit, a 501(c)(3), that Gordon Bellamy and Brian Rubin-Sowers started around 2006. It celebrates excellence and serves people across backgrounds. Its LGBTQ+ lens helps it center underestimated talent.

20years gathering at GDC, as of March 2026
602guests at the alcohol-free game night, GDC 2026
90+alumni of the graduate scholars program
2scholar programs, one undergraduate and one graduate
Why this is on your deskYou have read the books at banks and run the floor at studios. Very few people our scholars meet have done both.
What we are not askingNo check, no logo, no announcement. This is about you, as a person, in a room with people starting out.
What is in hereFive small ways in. Each takes about an hour. Exhibit D shows what an hour reaches.
