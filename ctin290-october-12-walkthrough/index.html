10:00 15 min

Standup, Two Minutes a Team, Standing Up

Each team says three things and nothing else: what moved since last session, what is blocked, and what they will have done by the next one. Standing keeps it to two minutes. Write every blocker on the board where it stays visible for the rest of the session, because a blocker nobody wrote down is a blocker that gets discovered in week fourteen. If two teams name the same blocker, stop and solve it once, in front of everybody.

10:15 15 min

Team culture exercise (replaces the long opening)

0 to 3: each person privately picks a preference (quiet thinking or talking through; written or spoken feedback; exploring or planning). Optional: something outside class they value. Anyone may pass. 3 to 6: "What helps me contribute is..." in any mode. 6 to 11: write one agreement, "We value ___; when ___, we will ___; evidence will be ___", and apply it to one real frame, sketch or timeline choice. 11 to 14: everyone marks works for me, change needed, or talk privately; silence is not agreement. 14 to 15: name the next artifact, its owner, a supporter, the blocker.

11:02 60 min

Team Project Time, and Where You Actually Stand

This is the block the longer session buys you, and it is the single biggest change to the course. Teams work. You rotate, and you rotate on a clock rather than by instinct, because otherwise you will spend the whole block with the team that is most fun to talk to. Fifteen minutes with each team, in a fixed order, and start with a different team every session so the same group is not always last and tired. With each team do three things: look at the build running rather than at a description of it, name the single most likely reason this misses the deadline, and agree one thing that will be true by next session. A team given nothing but freedom and a deadline will drift, and drift stays invisible until week fourteen, when it surfaces as weak work. Unstructured work time is not the fix for that. Structured work time with an instructor in the room is.

Rotation today (start with a different team than Wednesday): 11:02 Team S, 11:17 Team C, 11:32 Team U, 11:47 recovery and common fixes. Per team: watch the build run, name the single most likely reason it misses the deadline, agree one thing true by Wednesday.

12:02 33 min

Screening & Feedback

All three tracks screen their 30-second proof-of-concepts. Audience feedback: what worked? What's the next step? Discuss technical and creative decisions. Homework: refine based on feedback. Next: the artifact each team named.

12:35 15 min

Close, Blockers Out Loud

Back to the board and the blockers you wrote at the start. Which ones moved, which did not, and which have quietly become somebody else's problem. Each team says one sentence on what will be true by the next session, and you write it down so it can be checked. Then say what lands next session and what is due on Thursday, because deadlines in this class are on Thursdays at 5:00 PM PT and never at the door.
