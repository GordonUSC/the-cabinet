Today's three termsseen before defined, one question each

Blocking

Definition

The choreography of character and camera movement within a scene; physical positioning and trajectory.

What to look at

Bodies at a table. Who leans in, who pulls back, and when.

Where you see it

Sample any 10 min
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Mbynm0pGX0

Ask the room

Who moved last, and what did that movement tell you before anybody spoke?

Gesturality

Definition

The expressive quality of movement; how characters move and what that movement communicates about state, intent, or personality.

What to look at

One performer, many selves, distinguished almost entirely by how they move.

Where you see it

Throughout, 2:26
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wxN1T1uxQ2g

Ask the room

If you muted this, how many of these people could you still tell apart?

Reaction Shot

Definition

A shot of a character responding to action; often cut in for emotional emphasis or continuity.

What to look at

The table's faces are the story as often as the speaker is.

Where you see it

Sample any 10 min
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Mbynm0pGX0

Ask the room

Whose face is the story right now, and is it the person talking?
