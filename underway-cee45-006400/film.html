The words in the film

In 1983, an admiral was paid $250 for a lecture. He and Joann DiGennaro planted it.

Underway is a game for the 45th anniversary of the Center for Excellence in Education. It lets anyone at the reunion live an RSI summer, one tap at a time.

First, a week of classes, where you learn the one move: connect an idea to the evidence.

Then, five weeks in the lab with a mentor. Insight and rigor multiply. In week four, the experiment fails, as it always does. You choose what to learn from it.

On Presentation Day, the room applauds.

And your light joins the others: about 3,400 Rickoids, from all 50 states and 63 nations.

The summer ends where Rickoids know it does. At the holiday party, with a wall of thank-yous for Mrs. D, and the whole team at CEE.

You cannot lose. You can play, or you can just watch.

Underway. Every Rickoid's summer, played together.

A first draft by Gordon Bellamy, RSI '87. The narrator is a stock voice, not a real person's. No alumni names, likenesses or quotes appear; those come later, with each person's permission.
