The Care Between is fictional, AI-generated teaching imagery. It does not depict students, a real person, or recorded footage.

What produced the images

The image production process generated the greenhouse sequence and A/B framing study using Higgsfield GPT Image 2.5. The visual asset package includes its prompts and shot plan. It supplied eight widescreen images, 928 × 522 pixels each. The kit preserves their sequence and uses 4, 3, 4, 3, 4, 3, 4 and 5 second holds.

What this workshop adds

Shot annotations, image sequencing, the 30-second cut, descriptive text, accessibility controls, classroom exercises and timing. Image-generation imperfections remain fair material for critique. Ask what the frame actually proves. Do not infer an exact growth interval or a real botanical result.

How to discuss authorship

Separate the origin of an asset from a claim about legal ownership. Copyrightability depends on sufficient human expressive contribution and the facts of the work. Prompting alone does not establish authorship under the U.S. Copyright Office's guidance. A tool or workflow tier does not automatically make an output copyrightable.
