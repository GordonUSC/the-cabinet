Help more donors help more people.
A three-minute narrative for Sinjin to adapt and present
September 18, 2026

We have something valuable to bring to this work: relationships with people who want to help. GGP's Directory and Pipeline can give those relationships a clearer purpose. Donors can see where they fit, programs can explain what they need, and graduate students can find both an opportunity and someone willing to help them pursue it.

The human ambition is substantial. GGP's next cohort target is 40 graduate scholars, up from 21 last year. That is 19 more people and about 90% growth. At $1,500 each, the direct awards would total $60,000. The Directory can extend useful support beyond the award recipients, while the proposed Pipeline adds personal introductions and follow-through.

The donor prototype already offers ways to find and compare programs, follow sources, plan possible support and record giving with reported results. Alongside our existing tools and client relationships, that can make a funding conversation more useful: What matters to this donor? Which program fits? What is the verified need? Who should meet whom? We would confirm the recipient, use of funds and reporting before any gift.

I would start with a small, practical test. Review five records with three trusted partners, then test the graduate experience. Recruit five industry mentors for ten consenting graduate students. Two conversations per person would mean twenty conversations, with feedback on a portfolio or career plan. Those are proposed service goals we can check and learn from.

Take-Two is the inaugural lead sponsor, with $30,000 approved for Directory development and deployment. Bigglesworth could help bring aligned funders, reviewers and mentors into the work, and consider a separately costed contribution to keeping it useful. That conversation remains separate from the existing CYSTEM request.

The longer ambition is 1,000 graduate users and 100 people taking a useful next step by September 2027. We should refine those goals after the pilot and count people carefully. An application submitted, feedback received or a mentor meeting completed tells us more than a page view.

I am asking the board to help shape this first test, open the right relationships and review a costed plan before considering funding. Our opportunity is to empower more donors to help more people, and make the effect of that help visible.

Basis: Alex Rogers, 2025-26 CYSTEM Highlights; September 10-12 Directory correspondence; current prototype reviewed September 18; Gordon's September 18 direction. All future counts are proposed targets. Donor entries are self-reported; the prototype does not process donations or connect accounts.
