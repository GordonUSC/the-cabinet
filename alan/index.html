01

FRIENDSHIP → A WORLD

Gordtopia

What if preparing to play together were part of the adventure?

Eric and I share a Minecraft world. The portal connects our friendship to an Atlas, a Hold for organizing our materials, and a Fieldbook for deciding what to do next.

  1. Choose Meet us at the portal, then Leap in together.
  2. Explore a landmark in the Atlas and carry it into the Fieldbook.
  3. Try a world style, or open the separate cooperative adventure.
The design question

Do the characters and shared decisions make partnership meaningful? Where does presentation become a useful ritual for two people who keep building a world together?

Minecraft companion + separate Phaser game. Solo with a companion or two people on one keyboard. No remote multiplayer or shared notes service in this review copy.

Enter the portal ↗Play The Light We Leave →
Companion tools use local progress. They do not connect to the Minecraft server.
02

A PERSONAL ARCHIVE → PARTICIPATION

Take the
game-show seat.

The stories are mine. The next turn is yours.

My television appearances become a place to watch, try a question, give a clue, and explore how a group makes a decision. The actual record stays distinct from what a visitor chooses.

  1. Try one Russian Roulette practice question.
  2. Switch to Million Dollar Mind Game and explore the six-seat vote.
  3. Visit Watch to meet the original moment.
The design question

What changes when memory becomes something another person can do? Is the boundary between recorded history and a new play session clear?

Nine documented appearances, three main-game wins. Some media use outside services; this collection’s clips and core games open here without an account.

Take a turn ↗Watch the source moment →
Untimed by default. Taboo is for a clue-giver and a guesser together.

CLEARER INFORMATION → MORE OPPORTUNITY

Find the future
you want to fund.

Help good intentions become informed next steps.

The Directory and Pipeline bring programs, education stages, support relationships, and evidence into view. Donors are the first customer. This is an information service in development.

  1. Select Compare + on two or three programs.
  2. Explore Public giving and follow the evidence.
  3. Try My giving to see a sample portfolio and allocation.
The design question

Can someone understand the path from learning to making work, and see where support could help? What information must be maintained before this can become a dependable service?

Review prototype. No payments, applications, placements, participant database, or verified impact attributed to a donor’s sample entries. Target launch: Tuesday, March 2, 2027, during GDC week.
