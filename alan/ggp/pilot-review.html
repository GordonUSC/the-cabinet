AN OPERATING RHYTHM TO AGREE

Notice. Verify. Fix. Learn.

Recognize and triage

Record the page/record, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, actual result and impact. Separate a usability idea from a factual error or blocking defect.

Respond by impact

Exposure of private data, materially false claims or a broken core task should pause affected use or release. Assign other defects and improvements to an agreed review queue.

Recheck the fix

Retest the task, review the evidence, record what changed and update the release notes. Escalate unmet capacity rather than quietly allowing the service to decay.

Suggested cadence, subject to funded capacity
  • During the pilot: review issues after every session; repeat the core tasks after fixes.
  • Weekly during active use: triage corrections and broken links; check near-term deadlines and changed eligibility first.
  • Monthly: review a record sample, actual hours, unresolved issues and upcoming program cycles.
  • Before any release or audience expansion: independent quality review and a documented decision.

Cadence and response times must be agreed before they are promised. Each record needs a next-review date appropriate to how quickly its information changes.
