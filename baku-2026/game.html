Three laps of a Baku-shaped street circuit: the castle squeeze, the shoreline straight, the walls that end races. Your half of the screen is your cockpit, top and bottom in portrait, left and right sideways. Lower left or right to steer, hold both to brake, tap the top of your half to deploy Manual Override for a burst of energy. Track assist keeps you off the walls; Pro mode turns it off and raises the top speed.

Gordon's challenge: be the best, and be the most fabulous. Fastest to the chequered flag wins. Most style points, earned by running the walls at speed without touching them, wins Most Fabulous. Track limits are policed by the stewards. Nothing here knows how the real race went; that is yours to watch.

Pit wall vocabulary

Formation lap: the countdown before the five red lights. Lights out: when they go out, you go. Sector: the lap is cut in three; a purple sector is the fastest anyone has set. Delta: your gap to the other car in seconds. Manual Override: the 2026 energy boost, limited per lap, recharges on the straight. Box, box: pit call; there is no pit here, you are on the hard tyre all day. Track limits: touch a wall and the stewards note it. Chequered flag: the end. Parc ferme: results are final and nobody touches the cars.
