How to read a card

Each card carries the three things funders ask first. A lit seal means a sourced number backs it.

  • ✦ Tangible impact people reached, work made, growth
  • ✦ Direct to people money that lands with a person, or cost per person
  • ✦ Track record years running, public filings

A dashed box means no sourced number yet. The deck says so instead of guessing.
