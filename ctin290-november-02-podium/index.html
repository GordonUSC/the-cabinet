Notes to listen for

  • A board that needs the team to narrate it is not final. The tell-it-back test shows that in one minute.
  • "What we hear" should be on every shot. Beta is final sound, so sound decisions start now.
  • Any team still on initial-storyboard level leaves with a dated plan, not a grade.
