For Eric · Stoughton, Wisconsin

Dragon House in Blocks

A voxel build of the Iverson-Johnson House, the 1898 Queen Anne by architect A. E. Ovren that Eric is restoring at 327 E. Washington St. Tap the glowing blueprint to lay it course by course, from the foundation to the roof peak and its Dragon House flag. Paint the sash in James, Henry, and Oscar's colors. Switch to night and the windows light one by one while a dragon-prow circles the house.

A sign in the yard reads "Gordtopia: All Roads Lead Home," and a counter welcomes 3,425 villagers home, in honor of the 3,425 villagers saved at Minecraft Experience Rosemont on 6/27.

Home, built block by block. Restored by Eric.
